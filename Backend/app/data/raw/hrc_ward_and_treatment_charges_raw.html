Ward and Treatment Charges

The payment information below is important for you to know the financial implications before you are admitted to the ward and receive treatment.

There are two types of rooms available in each ward, namely First Class Room (single or double room) and Third Class Room.

Any investigation (example: blood test, x-ray, etc.) or medical equipment (example: orthosis, prosthesis, etc.) will be charged a separate fee based on the circular by the Ministry of Health Malaysia (MOH).

For employer -borne patients, please bring a valid Guarantee Letter (GL) from the employer before you are admitted to the ward or when you attend any appointments.

For paid patients, you will be notified of the amount of fees that may be charged the day before discharge. You can also be informed of the latest amount of your bill on a regular basis if you have been in the hospital for a long time.

Note: Please bring these documents for our verifications (if applicable):

  • Disabled (OKU) Card
  • Pension Card
  • School Letter


How to make a payment?

For your convenience, we accept payments in the form of cash, postal money orders, bank drafts, and bank checks. Personal checks are not accepted.

Please contact the Cheras Rehabilitation Hospital Revenue Unit for more information if you have any concerns regarding payment.
