# NSS 80th Round Health Survey Data

Official catalog: https://microdata.gov.in/NADA/index.php/catalog/290

Reference ID: `DDI-IND-NSO-HSCHealth80R-Jan2025-Dec2025`

- `raw/` contains byte-identical public-use CSV files `hhscsL1.csv` through `hhscsL7.csv`.
- `documentation/nss80_health_schedule.pdf` is the official Schedule 25.0 questionnaire/codebook.
- `source_metadata.json` is the official catalog metadata export.
- `processed/nss80_inpatient_model.csv` is generated by `model_training/nss80/train_model.py`; raw files are never overwritten.

The primary target is Level 4 / Block 7 item 12 (`b7i12`), total medical expenditure in whole Indian rupees for an inpatient case. Predictor meanings are decoded from the official schedule. The payment-safe v2 estimator collapses service payment categories to received/not received, collapses free versus paying-general wards, and excludes the free-medical-service field. The preparation audit, hashes, exclusions, and cohort counts are recorded in `Backend/models/nss80/metadata.json`, `Backend/reports/nss80/DATASET_INSPECTION_REPORT.md`, and `Backend/reports/nss80/FEATURE_LEAKAGE_AUDIT.md`.
